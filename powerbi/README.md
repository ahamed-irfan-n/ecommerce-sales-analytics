
# Power BI Dashboard | E-Commerce Sales Analytics

An interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze e-commerce sales performance and uncover insights into revenue trends, product categories, regional sales, payment methods, and customer ratings.

## Dashboard Preview

![Power BI E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Business Objectives

- Monitor key sales performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare revenue across product categories and regions.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Evaluate customer ratings across product categories.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Orders | Total number of orders placed |
| Total Customers | Number of unique customers |
| Units Sold | Total quantity of products sold |
| Total Revenue | Overall revenue generated |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue per order |

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis
A column chart showing monthly revenue trends to help identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 2. Revenue by Product Category
A donut chart comparing revenue contributions from Electronics, Clothing, Home, and Beauty.

### 3. Revenue by Region
A horizontal bar chart comparing revenue across the East, West, North, and South regions.

### 4. Orders by Payment Method
A donut chart illustrating the distribution of orders across Card, Cash on Delivery (COD), and Wallet payments.

### 5. Customer Rating by Category
A column chart comparing average customer ratings across product categories.

## Interactive Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes the following slicers:

- **Order Date** — Filter sales by date range.
- **Product Category** — Explore individual product categories.
- **Region** — Analyze sales across geographical regions.
- **Payment Method** — Compare different payment methods.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **CSV Dataset** — Source data for the dashboard
- **Data Analytics** — KPI reporting and sales performance analysis

## How to Access the Dashboard

1. Download `ecommerce_sales_dashboard.pbix` from this folder.
2. Open the file in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If necessary, update the data source path to your local cleaned CSV file.
4. Interact with the slicers to explore sales performance.

## Project Outcome

This dashboard presents e-commerce sales data through interactive visualizations and KPIs, making it easier to explore revenue trends, category performance, regional contributions, payment preferences, and customer ratings.
